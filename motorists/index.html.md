# Traafik For Motorists

> Motorist-facing landing page for the public Traafik website.

URL: https://www.traafik.com/motorists/

This page presents Traafik as a way for motorists to experience traffic stops in a safer, faster, and more digital format.

## Core Positioning

- Audience: motorists
- Main message: make traffic stops less tedious and a lot safer
- Specific public claim: the page says motorists can streamline an entire traffic stop in less than 10 minutes

## Public Features And Claims

- Digital interaction with law enforcement from a distance during traffic stops
- Less intrusive traffic-stop experience
- Quick citation processing
- Digital verification and exchange of motorist and law-enforcement profiles
- Past ticket payment in the app
- Citation routing to the proper municipalities
- Optional instant citation payment
- Payment gateways named on the page: Stripe, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, and more
- Voice-activated API guidance during the stop
- Claimed integration with law-enforcement IT and RMS systems

## Conversion Path

- Signup form fields: name and email
- CTA label: `Join the list`
